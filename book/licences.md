# Sources and licences

The book's own text and code are licensed under the [MIT licence](https://github.com/pietroppeter/nanuq-book/blob/main/LICENSE).

Ported chapters keep the licence of their source. Each one says so in the box at its top,
together with its authors and what the port changed. A source is only ported when its
licence allows it; sources without a licence are used as a list of topics at most.

| Source | Licence | Chapters | Data |
| ------ | ------- | -------- | ---- |
| [Polars user guide](https://docs.pola.rs/user-guide/), by the Polars contributors | [MIT](https://github.com/pola-rs/polars/blob/main/LICENSE) ([copy](https://github.com/pietroppeter/nanuq-book/blob/main/licenses/polars-MIT.txt)) | Expressions and contexts, Lazy API, Basic operations, Expression expansion, Casting, Missing data, Joins, Concatenation, CSV | `data/polars/` (Monopoly properties and the iris dataset, from the Polars repository; iris is originally from the UCI Machine Learning Repository, CC BY 4.0) |
| [polars-benchmark](https://github.com/pola-rs/polars-benchmark) (PDS-H queries), by the Polars contributors | [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/pola-rs/polars-benchmark/blob/main/LICENSE) ([copy](https://github.com/pietroppeter/nanuq-book/blob/main/licenses/polars-benchmark-Apache-2.0.txt)) | PDS-H queries | generated by [tpchgen-cli](https://github.com/datafusion-contrib/tpchgen-rs) (Apache-2.0) when the chapter runs, not stored |
| [One Billion Row Challenge](https://github.com/gunnarmorling/1brc), by Gunnar Morling and contributors | [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/gunnarmorling/1brc/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) ([copy](https://github.com/pietroppeter/nanuq-book/blob/main/licenses/1brc-Apache-2.0.txt)) | One Billion Row Challenge (the challenge and its generator; the chapter's text and code are the book's) | `data/1brc/weather_stations.csv` (the 413 stations of `CreateMeasurements.java`); measurements generated when the chapter runs |

The datasets used by the chapters are in the [data](https://github.com/pietroppeter/nanuq-book/tree/main/data)
folder, one subfolder per source, under the licence of that source.

The book is built with [nimibook](https://github.com/pietroppeter/nimibook), whose theme
and assets have their own licences (MPL-2.0 theme, fonts under Apache 2.0 and SIL OFL 1.1,
highlight.js under BSD-3-Clause, clipboard.js and KaTeX under MIT).
